# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/)
version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Standards

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery,
trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, public or private
harassment, publishing others' private information, and other conduct
that could be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Report incidents to the project maintainers. All reports will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
